    Why these rules

    • Meter errors, not trips. Some raw trips last days (one runs for over 22 days). Trips over 3 hours or under 1 minute are dropped from training and the clean test.
    • Impossible geometry. Pickups or drop-offs outside New York City, trips that never move, and straight-line speeds over 100 km/h are dropped.
    • Scored both ways. The Model page also scores every June trip, dirty rows included, so the cleaning cannot flatter the result.
    Data: NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission trip records via Kaggle's NYC Taxi Trip Duration competition. Built with pandas, LightGBM and plain SVG/Canvas.
